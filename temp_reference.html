From Ibarra to Africa

Born in the rolling hills of Ibarra, Basque Country, my journey began at the family farm Bizkardo baserria, where my mother and grandmother taught me the essence of customer excellence through their authentic dedication to their craft.

My father built resilience into my character, preparing me for the challenges that would define my path. These early lessons in Lan Egiati (honest work) became the foundation for everything that followed.

For 23 years, Africa has been calling my name. What started as a transformative journey to Kenya and Tanzania in 2002 has evolved into a mission to revolutionize financial experiences across the continent, shaped by formative experiences in gamification and user behavioral change.

The Foundation

Discovering Product Sense Through Gamification

My journey into understanding user behavioral change began at Karmacracy, a pioneering Spanish startup founded by Alex Dolara that revolutionized content sharing through gamification. Working alongside Alex, I discovered the profound impact of game mechanics on user engagement and behavior modification.

At Karmacracy, I learned to be a power user of gamification principles, understanding how elements like karma points, levels, badges, and social competition could drive authentic user engagement. Alex Dolara became an instrumental mentor who brought product sense and gamification expertise into my professional development, teaching me that successful products don't just solve problems—they create compelling experiences that users want to return to.

The platform's innovative approach combined URL shortening with sophisticated gamification mechanics, rewarding users for sharing quality content while building a community of engaged 'karmanautas.' This experience taught me that user behavioral change happens not through force, but through carefully designed incentive systems that align user motivations with business objectives.

This foundation in gamification principles would later prove invaluable in my payment solutions work, where understanding user psychology and behavioral triggers became essential for driving adoption across diverse African markets. Alex's mentorship helped me understand that great product management combines technical execution with deep empathy for user motivations and desires.

The Summit

Completed the prestigious World Cup mountain marathon in 7:49:46, finishing 395th overall. This 42.2km race with 2,853m elevation gain exemplifies my philosophy: 'No mountain is high enough when you combine authentic leadership with strategic vision.'
